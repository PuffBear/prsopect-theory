# SNR mechanism: diagnosed negative (full 180-run sweep, 150k horizon)

**Verdict: the phase boundary is REPRODUCED, but the mean-field Langevin/SNR
mechanism is NOT observable in the policy's training trajectory on this
substrate. The mechanism stays "sign-and-form", not predictive.**

(`snr_summary.md` is regenerated by `analyze_snr.py` on every run; this file is
the durable diagnosis and does not get overwritten.)

## What the sweep confirmed
- 180/180 runs landed, full w-grid {0.50..0.90} x 20 seeds.
- Collapse fraction is a clean monotone sigmoid: 1.00 @ w=0.50 -> 0.15 @ w=0.90.
- **Collapse-fraction=0.5 crossing = 0.822**, matching the horizon-matched
  empirical structural boundary (w_crit @150k = 0.822). The sweep independently
  re-derives the boundary. That part is solid.

## What failed, and why (not a tuning issue)
The restoring-drift / SNR crossing does NOT land at w_crit:
- Restoring-drift sign change w = 0.535 [0.502, 0.797]; empirical 0.822 is
  OUTSIDE the CI -> test reads False.
- Root cause from the trajectories (`trajectories/run_*.npz`): the observable's
  basin geometry does not exist in the data. The analyzer assumes a cooperative
  well at mu=-0.40 and a lazy well at mu=-1.0 with a barrier between. In fact:
  - survivors settle at mu ~ -0.20..-0.31 (NOT -0.40);
  - collapsers settle at mu ~ -0.18..-0.34 -- the SAME region as survivors;
  - nothing approaches mu=-1.0 (min mu reached ~ -0.50);
  - so the "contested band" (-0.95, -0.45) samples a region the paths barely
    enter, and survivors vs collapsers are not separated along mu at all.

## Salvage paths tested (all flat -- mechanism not recoverable by re-tuning)
Every observable saved in the trajectory data was checked for survivor-vs-
collapser separation (gap = survivor_mean - collapser_mean):

| observable                        | gap      |
|-----------------------------------|----------|
| sigma (exploration scale) mean    | -0.0029  |
| sigma early / end                 | +0.001 / -0.005 |
| mu variance (diffusion proxy)     | -0.0014  |
| mu min (excursion depth)          | +0.0153  |
| mu end                            | -0.0301 (wrong sign) |

None separate the outcomes; sigma and mu-variance are also flat in w. The
collapse outcome is a full-horizon mean_S+profit property that the early/mid
policy statistics do not encode. This is a STRUCTURAL finding, not a mis-set
window or mu_coop.

## Bottom line
- Boundary: REPRODUCED (collapse-frac crossing = 0.822). Keep as an independent
  confirmation of the phase boundary.
- Mechanism: the SNR/Langevin coordinate (mean action mu) does not carry the
  collapse signal here; demote to sign-and-form. Do NOT spend more compute on
  observable re-tuning -- the signal is absent from the trajectory, not hidden.
